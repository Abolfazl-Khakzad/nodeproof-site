Privacy notice

This notice explains how personal data is processed when you visit nodeproof.it or contact us by email or through the contact form, in accordance with art. 13 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data controller

Abolfazl Khakzad (NodeProof), Turin, Italy
Email: abolfazl@nodeproof.it

2. What data we process

  • Emails you send us: your name, email address, company, and anything you include in the message.
  • Contact form: your name, company, email address, product (if you provide it) and message.
  • Technical data: GitHub Pages, our hosting provider, may log IP addresses and browser data for security and to deliver the site. We do not access or use these logs.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. This site does not use cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, and does not load fonts or other resources from external servers.

3. Purpose and legal basis

  • Replying to your requests (by email or contact form) and discussing our services: art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps taken at your request before entering into a contract) and art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries).
  • Site security and delivery (hosting logs): art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating a secure website).

4. Recipients and processors

  • Aruba S.p.A. (Italy): email hosting.
  • GitHub, Inc. (USA): website hosting.
  • Forminit (UXPLUS LTD, United Kingdom): contact form processing. Form data is stored in data centres in the EU.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

5. Transfers outside the EU

GitHub, Inc. may process technical data in the USA, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Forminit is a UK company. The United Kingdom is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission. Contact form data is stored in the EU.

6. Retention

Emails and contact form submissions are deleted after 24 months unless a business relationship follows. In that case, they are kept for as long as required by Italian law (for example, tax and accounting rules).

7. Your rights

Under arts. 15–22 GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability. To exercise them, write to abolfazl@nodeproof.it. We reply within one month.

8. Complaints

You can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it).

9. Providing data

Providing your data is voluntary. Without your email address we cannot reply to you. Fields marked with * in the contact form are required. We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling.

10. Changes

We may update this notice. The current version is always published on this page.
